Three moves. No excuses.

1

Make the oath

State a goal that can be checked online, a deadline, a stake and who gets it if you slip.

2

Do the thing

Post your public proof as you go: a repo, a page, a published link. Check in whenever you like.

3

Validators decide

At the deadline they read your proof. Kept means a full refund. Broken means your stake is paid out.
